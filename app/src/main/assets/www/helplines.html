Before You Call

What to expect on the call.

Calling for help can feel intimidating. Here's generally what happens so you know what to expect.

  • 1You'll be asked what happened — share as much or as little as you're ready to.
  • 2They'll guide you on evidence, immediate safety, and next steps.
  • 3You may be referred to the right portal or office to file a formal complaint.
