Your Data

Privacy Policy

Cyber Hayat is built to help you safely, without adding to your risk. This page explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use this site.

Our commitment

All data and information you share on Cyber Hayat — an incident report, a chatbot conversation, a story, an ambassador application, or a settings preference — is kept under proper safety and proper guard: access is limited to the Cyber Hayat support team, transmission happens over encrypted HTTPS, and we only ever ask for the minimum needed to help you.

1. What gets recorded, and why

When you submit an incident report, a story, or a campus ambassador application, or when you have a conversation with the Chatbot, that content — along with the email you provide — is sent securely to a Google Sheet maintained by the Cyber Hayat support team. This lets a real person review urgent cases, follow up with you, and where appropriate, help connect you with the right authorities (such as FIA or NCCIA). Nothing is submitted until you've given consent through the one-time email prompt described below.

2. The one-time email & consent prompt

The first time you interact with any report form, the community forms, or the Chatbot, you'll be asked for your email address and asked to confirm you understand how it's used. This happens only once per device/browser — after that, every report you submit and every chatbot conversation you have is automatically linked to that same email, and you won't be asked again.

3. Confirmation emails

After you submit a report, story, or application, you'll receive a confirmation email from our Cyber Hayat inbox. When you start a new conversation with the Chatbot, you'll receive one email per conversation letting you know it's been logged for your safety — not one email per message. These emails also remind you how to reach a helpline immediately if you're in danger.

4. Chatbot conversations & emergency support

Once you've given consent, each exchange with the Cyber Hayat Assistant (your message and its reply) is logged to our support Sheet alongside your email and a conversation ID. Conversations that mention immediate danger or self-harm are flagged internally so our team can prioritize a follow-up. This exists so that if you reach out in a moment of crisis, a real person — not just an AI — can also see it and act. If you'd rather not have a conversation logged, please use the Helplines page to call directly instead.

5. Google Gemini

If you add your own Gemini API key in Settings, your chatbot messages are also sent directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API using that key, to generate a reply. That traffic is governed by Google's own privacy policy, separately from the conversation logging described above.

6. What stays local only

Your dark mode, language, and Gemini key preferences are stored only in your browser's local storage and are never sent anywhere. The password strength checker never transmits or stores the password you type — it's scored entirely on your device.

7. Who can access the records

Submitted reports and chatbot logs are only accessible to the Cyber Hayat team members who manage the support Sheet and the dedicated Cyber Hayat email account — not published, not sold, and not shared with any other third party except where required to connect you with official authorities at your request.

8. Changes to this policy

If how we handle your data changes, this page will be updated first, with a clear explanation of what's different.

Questions about your data? Reach out through the Helplines page or our Instagram.
