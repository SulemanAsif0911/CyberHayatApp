Using This Platform

Terms of Use

Cyber Hayat exists to help people stay safe from cyber harassment. These terms keep that mission intact for everyone who uses it.

Ethical use comes first

By using Cyber Hayat, you agree to cooperate with other members of this platform ethically and not to misuse it. That means no harassment, impersonation, spam, exploitation of the reporting tools, or any use of this platform to target, intimidate, or harm someone else — the exact behavior Cyber Hayat exists to fight.

1. What this platform is

Cyber Hayat is a student-led awareness and self-help resource. It is not a law firm, a government agency, a licensed counseling service, or an emergency responder. Tools like the incident report drafter, complaint letter generator, and chatbot are aids for organizing your own next steps — not official submissions or legal advice.

2. Acceptable use

You agree to use Cyber Hayat only for genuine cyber-safety awareness, self-help, and reporting purposes. You will not use the chatbot, forms, or community features to harass, defame, threaten, or expose another person, submit false reports, or attempt to disrupt or exploit the platform's tools or connected services (including the Gemini API or Google Sheets sync).

3. Community & ambassador conduct

If you take part as a campus ambassador, story contributor, or community member, you agree to treat other members, survivors, and the Cyber Hayat team ethically and respectfully at all times, and to represent the platform's mission honestly in any outreach you do on its behalf.

4. No guarantee of outcome

Cyber Hayat cannot guarantee that a report will be resolved a particular way, that a platform or authority will act by a certain time, or that the chatbot's guidance fits every situation. Always use your own judgment and, where possible, confirm details with a qualified professional or the relevant official channel.

5. Third-party services

Features that use Google Gemini or Google Sign-In/Sheets rely on your own API key or Google account and are subject to Google's terms. Cyber Hayat is not responsible for the availability, accuracy, or policies of these third-party services.

6. Changes

These terms may be updated as the platform grows. Continued use of Cyber Hayat after an update means you accept the current version of these terms.

If you are experiencing or witnessing misuse of this platform, or urgent danger, please use the Helplines page rather than waiting on any in-platform tool.
