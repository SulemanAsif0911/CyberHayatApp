Action Path

Prefer to read? Here's the same guide path in three steps.

Before you block or delete anything, capture proof. This protects you if you need to file an official report later.
  • Screenshot messages, posts, profiles, and comments — include timestamps.
  • Save usernames, profile links, phone numbers, and email addresses involved.
  • Note the platform, approximate date, and a short description while it's fresh.
  • Do not delete or block until you've saved everything you can.
Once evidence is safe, lock down your accounts so the harassment can't continue or escalate.
  • Change passwords on affected accounts, and anywhere you reused that password.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication wherever it's offered.
  • Review privacy settings — who can message you, tag you, or see your posts.
  • Tell a trusted friend, family member, or colleague what's happening.
You don't have to handle this alone or figure out the system by yourself.
  • File through the FIA or NCCIA complaint portal, keeping the acknowledgement number.
  • Contact the Digital Rights Foundation helpdesk for guidance and emotional support.
  • Call a helpline if you need to talk to someone immediately — see the Helplines page.
  • Use the Chatbot for a step-by-step walkthrough tailored to your situation.
