Your path in three steps

Calm, practical and in the right order.

  1. 01

    Save the evidence

    Screenshot messages, profiles and links with the date and time visible. Note usernames and phone numbers. Don't delete or block until you have everything.

    ScreenshotsTimestampsUsernames
  2. 02

    Secure your accounts

    Change passwords, turn on two-step verification, review privacy settings and tell a trusted person what happened.

    Passwords2FAPrivacy
  3. 03

    Report and reach help

    Use the official complaint portals, cybercrime helplines and digital-rights support to move forward safely.

    FIANCCIADRF
